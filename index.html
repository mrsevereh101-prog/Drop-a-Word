Drop a Word

Creating words, building worlds

Letter blocks roll off the Word Loom one at a time. Stack them so they spell words, and the words clear away, just like lines in Tetris.

How to play
  • The conveyor shows the word being sent down, one letter at a time.
  • Spell a word of 3+ letters left to right, or up or down a column.
  • Words of 4+ letters also count backwards across and on diagonals. Diagonal words score ×1.5.
  • Longer words score more. Clearing one word that makes another is a chain.
  • If blocks stack to the top of the loom, the shift is over.
  • Tap or drag across the board to move the block, swipe down to drop it. Keys: ← → move, ↓ or space drop, P pause.
